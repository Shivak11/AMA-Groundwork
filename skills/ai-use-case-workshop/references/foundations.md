# Method foundations

This classroom method is Dr. Shiva Kakkar's synthesis, adapted from the existing Four-Gate GenAI Opportunity Lab and the shorter six-phase Hyundai Mobis workbook. The new package removes client-specific facts and the longer investment-planning gates. It retains the relationship between an outcome, the work that produces it, candidate changes and a justified next decision.

The source recap uses the existing sourcebook and consultant foundation notes. It does not claim a fresh full-text review of the books. Do not invent quotations or imply that any author endorsed this exact six-phase method.

The original method also drew on Shrestha, Ben-Menahem and von Krogh's work on organisational decision-making structures in the age of AI. Here, the retained practical question is who has the authority to act, check and respond when an output is wrong.

## Workshop-specific synthesis

TRACE is a difficult-case replay: take a recent case, reconstruct its events, include surrounding work, examine friction and identify the constraint. Participants do this in ordinary language before any framework name is introduced.

The zero-second test asks what would still prevent the outcome if a named task took no time. It tests whether the group has identified the real constraint. It does not prove that AI can remove that constraint.

The relationship between evidence, prediction, judgement, authorisation, action and accountability is a workshop synthesis. The six-phase version keeps the human check, proposed owner and stop rule visible without turning them into a separate theory lesson.

The candidate-to-task links, outcome measure and first-choice challenge are classroom adaptations. They support a reasoned discussion; they are not a validated quantitative measure of training effectiveness or an objective score of participants.

## What stays outside this exercise

The longer source method includes detailed investment cases, technical components, readiness assessments, workforce transition and extended evidence contracts. Those are useful follow-on work, but they are not required to finish this classroom book. Keep unknown baselines and costs explicit. A use case cannot become a verified saving or an authorised deployment merely because the group includes it in its portfolio.

Keep private transcript references, recording IDs, access logs and internal source paths outside the participant workbook. If asked about provenance, explain the foundations above in a short answer without exposing source records.
